# Who I Am

Hi, I'm Meghedi, a motivated English teacher and a polyglot who speaks 6 languages.

* **English:** C2, with a Teaching Training Certificate (TTC)
* **Persian (Farsi):** I am a native speaker, so if you speak this language too, it means we already have another language in common.
* **Armenian:** I am also a native Armenian speaker, so I share a second native language with millions of Armenian-speaking people around the world.
* **Turkish:** C1. I am quite a confident speaker of Turkish.
* **Azerbaijani:** C1. I have been learning the language from native speakers since I was a child.
* **Spanish:** A2. I am happily learning Spanish, one of the most widely spoken languages in the world.

# What I Teach

I help students build real confidence in English, whether they simply want to speak more fluently in everyday conversations or feel more confident using English in the workplace.

I also help people who struggle with making grammatically correct sentences in English and want to increase their confidence by improving their grammar.

# My Personal Side

Beyond learning and teaching languages which is not only my job but also my biggest hobby, I really enjoy spending time in nature. In my free time, I like going for walks in the woods and sipping my coffee in a park or by the seaside.

I enjoy watching movies in different genres, but my favourite one is sci-fi. Listening to music always helps me relax and get my mind off a hectic day.

I also love travelling, learning about new cultures, and collecting visual memories from historic and eye-catching places all around the world. So, if you want to learn English for travelling, it means we already have a mutual hobby to talk about for hours (and, of course, learn a lot of new words along the way!).

My biggest treasure in life is my family. I love spending time with them and enjoy any and every moment I can get with them.
